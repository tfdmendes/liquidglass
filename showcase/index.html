These measurements follow requestAnimationFrame callbacks. They describe the cadence the browser delivers, rather than a physical GPU frame counter.

What we compare

Previous is our first optimization pass. Current is our latest bundled engine. The optional original is our earlier fork at commit 862e553. Each build receives the same scene, lens count and animation.

How we measure

Builds warm up and run one at a time, across three alternating trials each. The browser and display refresh rate can cap FPS. Switching to a hidden tab invalidates the measurement.

What to look for

Higher FPS and lower frame intervals generally mean smoother animation. Update work is time spent updating the lenses. Missed slots estimate gaps against the measured refresh cadence.

Keep the evidence

Download the JSON to keep trial timings, settings and environment details together. A recorded report, when supplied, is separate from the benchmark you run on this device.
